Privacy Policy — Moon Pet

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application Moon Pet (the “App”) handles your information. Moon Pet is a cozy virtual-pet game. We have deliberately kept it simple: the App does not ask you to create an account, does not ask for your name, e-mail address or phone number, and does not upload your game progress to any server of ours.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, e-mail address, postal address or phone number;
  • Your contacts, calendar, photos, camera or microphone;
  • Your precise or approximate location;
  • Any account or login credentials — the App has no accounts.

2. Information Stored On Your Device

The App saves your game progress locally on your device using Android’s SharedPreferences. This includes your companion’s name and chosen species, its stats (satiety, energy, spirit), your streak, collected moon dust, purchased accessories, and your reminder time if you set one.

This data never leaves your device and is not visible to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the App.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Moon Pet is free to play and is supported by ads. The App uses the Google AdMob SDK to display rewarded video and interstitial ads. AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and process certain information in order to serve and measure ads, which can include:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID / AAID);
  • Device information such as model, operating-system version and language;
  • Your IP address, which may be used to infer coarse (city-level) location;
  • Ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed or clicked.

We do not receive this data in a form that identifies you. It is collected and controlled by Google. You can learn how Google uses it here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

On iOS, if you are asked to allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency), you may decline, and the App will still work fully — you will simply receive non-personalised ads. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time under Settings → Privacy → Ads.

4. In-App Purchases — RevenueCat

The App offers an optional premium subscription. Purchases are processed by Google Play Billing (on Android) or the Apple App Store (on iOS). We use RevenueCat to validate purchase receipts and to restore purchases across a reinstall.

We never see or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Google or Apple. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user identifier and the purchase receipt, and its own privacy policy applies: revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. Notifications

If you enable the nightly reminder, the App schedules a local notification on your device. This reminder is generated entirely on your device; no notification data is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn it off at any time in the App’s settings or in your device settings.

6. Children’s Privacy

Moon Pet is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App itself collects no personal information, there is nothing for us to delete — but if you believe a child has provided data through an advertising or purchase partner, please contact us and we will help you exercise the relevant partner’s controls.

7. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we hold nothing about you to access, correct or delete. The data that advertising and billing partners process is controlled by them; the links in sections 3 and 4 above let you review and control it. If you are in the EEA, UK or California and have a request, write to us at the address below and we will do our best to help.

8. Data Security

Your game data lives only on your device and is protected by the device’s own sandboxing. The App communicates with Google’s ad and billing services over encrypted HTTPS connections.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example if we add a new feature or a new third-party service. The “Last updated” date at the top will change accordingly. Continued use of the App after a change means you accept the updated policy.
